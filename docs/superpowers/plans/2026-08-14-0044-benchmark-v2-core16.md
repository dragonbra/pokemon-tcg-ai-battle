**Goal:** Replace the retired 28-Meta Benchmark V2 schedule with a fixed 16-Meta CUDA-2048 schedule over Meta 00–13, 17 and 27, then resume 0044 from its latest durable checkpoint with a new baseline.

**Architecture:** Keep Policy-0809, deployment materialization, CUDA Engine 2.0 and common-random seed semantics unchanged. Change only the schedule domain: allocate exactly 128 games to each selected Meta, then balance those 128 games across exact member decks with count spread at most one. Preserve the old report as retired evidence and use a new contract ID and repository/W&B version for continuation.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11, PyTorch, CUDA Engine 2.0, pytest, W&B.

## Global Constraints

- Selected Meta IDs are exactly `00`–`13`, `17`, and `27`.
- Total games are exactly 2,048: 128 per selected Meta.
- Within each selected Meta, member deck counts differ by at most one.
- Opponent policy remains complete immutable `Policy-0809`.
- Engine, Search, policy and coin-winner seeds remain fixed and independent of focal identity.
- Evaluation remains isolated from PPO and uniform Champion-G2 rollout sampling.
- Preserve all used run directories, checkpoints and reports; do not overwrite retired evidence.
