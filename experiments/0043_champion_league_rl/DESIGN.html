Scope

0043 changes opponent assets, league sampling, PFSP curriculum, telemetry, Frozen evaluation assetization and manual Champion governance. The approved actor/value architecture, PPO loss and optimizer contract, 256-game rollout, action semantics and official engine remain unchanged.

Authoritative contracts

The project conforms to the 0043 Charter, Promote Champion V2 and repository-wide Frozen Policy Protocol V1. General official rules, current card/engine facts and strategy hypotheses remain separate evidence layers.

The canonical deck identity is the zero-padded number: immutable 001–055, then append-only from 056. Archetypes, hashes and historical IDs are metadata. Directories use semantic IDs rather than hashes; SHA-256 remains an integrity field only.

Model and PPO

Frozen prototype/state/option representations feed the inherited action decoder, strategy/value adapters, value blocks, allocation head and optional prize auxiliary. The canonical config preserves FP32 PPO, three complete shuffled passes, minibatch 2,048, turn-clock GAE 0.95 and all approved learning-rate/KL/loss values.

PFSP uses Beta(1,1) smoothing and freezes each curriculum for ten updates. Joint deck × policy statistics are diagnostics, not a compatibility gate.

Evidence

Rollout WR, prize/turn splits and PFSP tails are training diagnostics. Formal strength evidence requires the exact FP16-storage/FP32-runtime candidate, independent full opponent identity, official-engine games and zero error/unfinished/fallback. Promotion remains an explicit human decision.

Stage

Phase 0 audit and Phase 1 registries are complete. Complete policy reconstruction/no-hybrid isolation, sampler, PFSP, PPO regression, telemetry, Frozen parity and Promote workflow remain hard gates before formal training.
