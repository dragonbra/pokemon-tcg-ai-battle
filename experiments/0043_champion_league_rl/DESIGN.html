Evidence

Rollout WR, prize/turn splits and PFSP tails are training diagnostics. Formal strength evidence requires the exact FP16-storage/FP32-runtime candidate, independent full opponent identity, official-engine games and zero error/unfinished/fallback. Promotion remains an explicit human decision.

FrozenMeta CPU-256 and CUDA-2048 are independently identity-bound schedules; CUDA contains eight immutable replicas and begins with the exact CPU frequency unit. Seeded toss still delegates context 41 first/second choice to the winning Agent.

The self-contained 0043 adapter verifies tracked source bytes, state/rule ABI, private rule-pack hash, SM120 capability, native binary, extension, and complete policy/deck identity before routing. The RTX 5080 production library, native smoke and PyTorch extension build and load successfully; official rule upload and a two-lane reset/classify smoke pass with state ABI 7 and rule ABI 1. One optional CUDA 12.8 continuation diagnostic translation unit reached roughly 30 GiB RSS by itself. The 0043 build entrypoint therefore builds only production targets ptcg_cuda_smoke and _ptcg_cuda, strictly with --parallel 1, and never invokes CMake all.
