Reward and PPO

Reward remains terminal win/draw/loss only at +1/0/-1, with no shaping and gamma=1.0. The audited 0023 selection clock applies lambda=0.95 between focal decisions; loss weighting is Episode-equal. PPO hyperparameters and trainable tensor boundaries are unchanged. V7 changes the environment schedule and RNG contract, not the reward or network shape.

The resident implementation keeps rollout features, ordered targets, complete-Episode filtering, terminal rewards, GAE, Episode-equal weights, advantage normalization and PPO minibatch indices on CUDA. Only scalar monitoring and model-only checkpoints cross to CPU, and terminal lanes refill in place with disjoint deterministic seeds. This changes runtime residency only, not model, action, reward, credit or loss semantics; it does not mean V7 has run.

Seeded pair contract

Every update samples 128 unique engine seeds and 128 opponent slots from the frozen 256-slot frame. Focal remains physical player 0 and opponent player 1; every seed produces adjacent focal-first and focal-second Episodes through the official setup choice. A pair shares exact decks, engine seed and Search seed. Stochastic policy actions use a distinct per-Episode torch.Generator. This is paired environment randomness, not action-indexed CRN after policies diverge.

Schedule and monitoring

Evaluation and RL explicitly lock workers to engine/build/seeded_official/0002/libcg.so; its manifest retains official-source, adapter, compiler and library hashes. Runtime 0002 preserves the 0001 official ABI and engine semantics while statically linking libstdc++/libgcc, so pure ctypes workers do not depend on PyTorch library-loading order. Training resamples 128 pairs by source-policy update, while greedy probes reconstruct one fixed 128-seed / 256-Episode suite across checkpoints. Final strength still requires a separate zero-error official-engine evaluation under identical runtime, schedule and policy hashes.
