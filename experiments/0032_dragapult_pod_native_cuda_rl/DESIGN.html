Model flow

Field embeddings and numeric GELU projections form global, entity and option tokens. Parent/child relations enrich entities; a four-layer, 8-head Transformer encodes global plus 128 entities. Two option cross-attention layers attend to state. The copied 0031 GRU pointer decoder emits unique option indices plus STOP for up to 64 steps. A two-layer head emits one scalar value.

Rollout and PPO

Official results map to focal reward as win +1, loss -1 and draw 0. Only complete Episodes enter terminal-return GAE with gamma 1.0 and lambda 0.95; incomplete boundary trajectories are discarded. Episodes receive equal total weight. V4 freezes the encoder and trains only the action decoder and value head with fresh AdamW, two PPO epochs, minibatch 512, clip 0.10, entropy 0.01, reference KL 0.02 and gradient clip 0.5. Behavior log probability must replay within 1e-4 before optimization.
