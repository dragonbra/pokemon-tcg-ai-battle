Compact checkpoints

Evaluation

Evaluation runs at U0 before training, then U5/U10/etc. It reloads the saved compact checkpoint into a fresh base and requires exact effective-hash equality with the live policy before official games. There are exactly two physical CUDA-512 pools:

  • focus_seven: 256 games balanced within old_three exact decks 001/002/011 plus 256 games balanced within new_four exact decks 007/003/009/023
  • remain_meta: every other exact deck, Meta-first/deck-balanced, CUDA 512

Reports and W&B expose focus, old-three, new-four, remain-meta, per-deck and per-meta results. router/meta_expert_heatmap is emitted at U0 and every later eval. All games use independent Frozen Policy-0814 and canonical FP16-storage/FP32-runtime materialization with source FP32, portable FP16 and deployment-effective identities. True scheduled Meta is never passed to the focal Router.
