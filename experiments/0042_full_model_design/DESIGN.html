Decision

0042 freezes the semantic geometry learned by 0031, trains the paired latent-query Value network, and lets policy read q1, Meta probabilities, and adapted Value through an explicit stop-gradient Strategy Adapter. Option Q/V LoRA and the dormant 23-class Meta conditioner are absent.

PPO Protocol V2 and Frozen-0809

Exact focal deck 007_dragapult_ex; 256 games per update; all valid decisions; 2,048-decision minibatches; up to three complete shuffled data epochs; target/hard behavior KL guards 0.015/0.025; FP32 AdamW; independently explicit actor-group LRs; Value-only LR 1e-4; no update cap.

The U250 identical-rollout diagnostic selected decoder 2e-5, Policy Adapter 4e-5, and allocation 2e-5. Across 22,719 decisions and three epochs it measured behavior KL 4.30e-4, root/macro KL 5.62e-5/2.36e-3, clip fraction 0.253%, and complete 3x reuse. The branch loads U250 focal weights after snapshotting Policy-0809/U0 as the immutable reference-KL policy; AdamW state and rollout data start fresh.

The independent V4 smoke completed 22,182 decisions, 33 optimizer steps, 100% coverage and 3x reuse with behavior KL 3.82e-4 and clip fraction 0.344%. V5 starts again from sealed U250, not from smoke U1, and runs without an update cap.

Each logical 2,048-decision optimizer step uses at most two 1,024-row physical graphs with one shared loss denominator, one accumulated gradient, one clip, and one AdamW step. This retains 11 logical steps for 21,203 decisions while avoiding the WSL DXG residency failure observed with a physical 2,048-row graph. A one-update validation completed 33 steps at 0.94 iter/s with peak allocated/reserved memory 6.78/8.52 GB.

The behavior guard is one deterministic set of 4,096 shuffled rollout rows plus every compound/macro row, fixed across epochs. Update 1 and every tenth update also audit pre-update old-logprob parity over every decision; other updates audit the guard set. Probe rows never count as optimized samples.

Training and evaluation opponents are separately materialized complete immutable Policy-0809 instances. Resident CUDA audits every job-role resource ledger against its own exact deck and rejects any batch-global deck substitution. The measured 256-game preflight covered all 55 exact decks, 512 job-role ledgers, 21,203 decisions, and zero bulk feature D2H. Ready lanes are compacted by actor role before each complete policy forward and scattered back afterward, so focal and opponent identities remain independent while neither model evaluates the other role's rows. A 46,061-decision parity benchmark passed exactly and improved throughput from 6.04 to 6.68 games/s (+10.6%). The production preflight reached 7.77 games/s; 1.119 GB of semantic features stayed on CUDA while only 2.84 MB of compact control/trajectory data moved to the host. PPO gathers from the contiguous CUDA feature store, and Phantom allocation evaluation is batched by allocation/target shape.

Update 0 and every 10 updates export FP16 candidate storage, strict-load FP32 runtime, audit effective identity, and run an identity-bound Frozen-0809 CUDA-2048 evaluation.

Deployment-effective identity hashes deployed FP16 tensors and runtime-semantic schema only. Source checkpoint/file hashes, update numbers, version names, and training provenance remain separate audit fields. U0 pins its corrected content identity and derived schedule; later checkpoints derive schedules from their own effective content identity.
